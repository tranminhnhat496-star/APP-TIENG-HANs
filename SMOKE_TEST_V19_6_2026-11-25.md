## What changed

Sentence Forge auto-generates sentences from the active word set (scope/learned words) instead of drawing only from the fixed 40-sentence bank. `genPool()` priority: learned-in-scope → learned-any → unlearned-in-scope (cap 12, session 5). `genItem()`: EX-EXTEND (real `ex` verbatim, extended by level) or POS templates (noun/verb/adjective), A1 simple → B2 which-clause; honest bank fallback for multi-word/capital/modal/attributive-only cases — never fabricated. KO prefers the selected deck, items stay verbatim `card.e`. Flag `forgeAuto` in `S.v7.en48.flags` (default ON). No scoring/XP/SRS/pet/save-schema change; no new save key. V50 score tracking and V52 TTS are preserved (regression-checked below).

NOT changed here (deliberate): OBS-1 (lead), the `forgeNext` double-call lead, the KO-streak quirk, and all other pending decision items — see CHANGELOG.

## How to smoke-test by hand (2 minutes)
1. Open the game → English Hub → Library → pick A1–A2 "Education" (learn a few words first if the set is fresh) → open Sentence Forge. The pre-session card must name "A1–A2 · Education – GIÁO DỤC" and a generated count.
2. Play a session: every generated question shows the "Câu tạo tự động" badge with its keyword. Switch the scope to Oxford Bài 12 — the next session's questions come from that lesson.
3. Turn the flag off (`window.__v48.setFlag('forgeAuto', false)` in the console) — the session is the old 40-bank pool with no badge; turn it back on and generated sentences return.
