## What to check by hand (Anh, on the live page after deploy)

1. **Legendary difficulty scales (the headline change):** Bosses → **Legendary Boss Arena** → tap **"Khiêu chiến"** on any Legendary boss. If you have mastered **<200 words**, questions feel as before (A2+B1 words). If you have mastered **200–599 words**, questions are B1-only — noticeably harder. At **≥600**, B2-only. The arena card's promise ("Độ khó câu hỏi tăng theo tiến trình học của bạn") is now true. If you have ≥200 mastered words and the questions suddenly feel harder, that is the intended change, not a bug.
2. **Korean boss answers pay XP:** fight any boss in **Tiếng Hàn** and answer correctly — your XP must go up (+10 on a new card, +5 more every 5th correct in a row). Before V19.0.1 it stayed at 0. Wrong answers still pay 0.
3. **Weakness meter fills:** fight **Từ Điển Bóng Tối** (Dark Dictionary, English arena) — the **💥 Điểm yếu: Giáo dục** bar must fill as you answer its education-topic words, and BREAK must fire at 3/3 (+bonus damage). Before V19.0.1 its weakness (fruit) had no B2 words in the pool, so the bar could never fill.
4. **First-win / replay rewards unchanged:** winning a boss the first time still pays the same reward as before (v18 slime +50 XP/+1 gem; v19 fireking +70 XP/+2 gems); replaying still pays +10 XP. Nothing was taken away.
5. **Nothing else looks different:** all bosses, farm, pets, XP/gold/diamonds, and your save must be exactly as before. No new currency, no new boss, no layout change (this version has no CSS changes at all).
