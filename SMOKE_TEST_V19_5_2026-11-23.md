## How to smoke-test by hand (2 minutes)
1. Open the game, go to English Hub → Bosses → Challenge Arena → Pháp Sư Phát Âm (Lv 8) → fight in English. On a speak question, tap "Nói", then tap the boss list to start the same fight again — the old mic session must not answer the new question.
2. Smart Learning → Từ yếu → filter "Đến hạn": a learned word with no due date must read "Đến hạn hôm nay", never a four- or five-digit day count.
3. Sentence Forge → play a session → the end screen must show "Điểm buổi: X điểm · Điểm cao nhất: Y điểm".
4. On a 360px-wide phone, open the Korean farm home: every button (header, "Người chơi", tab/row buttons, bottom tabs) must be at least 44px tall, with no horizontal scrolling.
