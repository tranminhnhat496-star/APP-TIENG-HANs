## How to smoke-test (5 minutes, no account needed)

1. Open `index.html` (or the GitHub Pages URL after deploy). The title bar reads **V18.7 — Unlock Shortcuts**. No login required — guest play works.
2. **Chrono shortcut (fresh save, 0 wins):** English Hub → Chrono Kingdom (locked, 0/70). The gate card shows a button **"⚔️ Đánh Nhớt từ vựng ngay"** (old slime, Lv 1 — the easiest unwon, fightable boss) with "…để lên 1/70." Tap it: the Chrono overlay closes and the slime fight opens (English questions). Win it: the count becomes 1/70.
3. **Chrono shortcut (KO mode):** switch the Chrono overlay to 🇰🇷 Tiếng Hàn while locked. The button must NOT name an old V15 boss (English-only) — it names a V18/V19 boss instead.
4. **Chrono shortcut (replay):** after winning a boss in one language, the V19 button must never offer that same boss again in the other language (it already counts toward the 70).
5. **Skyroot shortcut (70 wins, GEAR GOLEM unbeaten):** open Skyroot (locked). The gate card shows **"⚔️ Đánh Tinh Linh Đồng Hồ Cát ngay — mở đường tới GEAR GOLEM"** (first unlocked, unwon Chrono boss) or **"⚔️ Đánh GEAR GOLEM ngay"** once the first 7 are won. Tap it: the Skyroot overlay closes and the Chrono fight opens in the chosen language.
5b. **Skyroot shortcut (70-gate still closed — Anh's screenshot state, 13/86 wins, KO):** open Skyroot (locked). The gate card must NOT say the player has won 8 Chrono bosses. It shows a button **"⚔️ Đánh \<boss\> ngay — mở đường tới GEAR GOLEM"** naming the easiest unwon, fightable boss of the 70, with "\<boss\> · Lv \<lv\> · boss dễ nhất bạn chưa thắng — thắng trận này (tiếng Hàn) để lên 14/70, mở Vương Quốc Thời Gian rồi đánh bại GEAR GOLEM." Tap it: the Skyroot overlay closes and that boss's fight opens (NOT a Chrono fight — the 70-gate is still closed).
6. **Gate open:** after winning GEAR GOLEM in a language, the Skyroot gate card shows no shortcut button at all (gate open → `skyrootGateHTML` returns `''`). Same for Chrono at 70 wins.
7. **No dead buttons:** a locked overlay never shows a shortcut button that does nothing — when no target exists it shows an honest `<p>` instead.

## Fixed in this revision — the Skyroot/70-gate false line

- **Skyroot shortcut while the 70-win Chrono gate is closed now points at the Chrono shortcut.** At 13/86 wins (Anh's screenshot state) the old overlay showed "Đã thắng đủ 8 boss Chrono bằng ngôn ngữ này…" even though the player has NOT won 8 Chrono bosses. Now `skyrootTarget()` falls back to the easiest unwon, fightable boss of the 70 (`viaChrono:true`) — see step 5b above — and the null line is the honest "Không tìm thấy boss nào có thể đánh ngay — hãy đóng và mở lại Thánh Địa để cập nhật." (defensive-only).
