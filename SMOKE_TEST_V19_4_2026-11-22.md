## How to smoke-test by hand (2 minutes)
1. Open the game, go to English Hub → Bosses → Challenge Arena → Pháp Sư Phát Âm (Lv 8) → fight in English.
2. On a speak question, tap "Nói" and say the word shown. The card must show "Đang nghe… hãy nói: \<word\>" while listening, then mark the answer.
3. Deny the microphone permission (or test in a browser without a mic): tap "Nói" — the card must show a specific reason ("Micro bị chặn…") with a retry, not a frozen card.
4. Say nothing for a few seconds: the card must show "Máy không nghe thấy gì…" and let you tap "Nói" again.
