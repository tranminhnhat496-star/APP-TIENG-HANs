## How to smoke-test by hand (2 minutes)
1. Open the game, go to English Hub → Bosses → Challenge Arena → any boss → fight in English. Answer a grammar question and a sentence-builder question correctly — each must pay +10 XP (watch the XP counter) and move the streak; a full 4-question mixed fight pays 40 XP.
2. On an Android phone, tap any "Nghe" button (a word, a boss listen question, the Dojo) — it must speak. If the phone has no matching voice, it must show "Không phát được âm thanh — bấm Nghe lại." instead of staying silent; tapping "Nghe lại" retries.
3. On a 360px-wide phone, trigger the TTS error (e.g. airplane mode with no voices): the error toast must be fully visible with no horizontal scrolling.
