## What to check by hand (Anh, on the live page after deploy)

1. **Fresh player, Review tab:** open the game fresh (clear the save or a new profile) → English Hub → **Ôn tập** (Review). It must say **0 từ cần ôn hôm nay** and the button must read **Ôn tất cả từ cần ôn (0)**. Tapping it must show the honest pop **"Không có từ nào cần ôn hôm nay."** — NOT start a lesson with new words. Before V19.0 a fresh player was told 4,889 words "need review today".
2. **Learn a few words, then Review:** learn 2–3 English words (answer them so they enter box 1–4), wait for their due time or just check the counter — the Review tab must count ONLY words you have actually started learning, never brand-new words. Box-5 (mastered) words past due must NOT be counted as "cần ôn".
3. **Topics tab:** legacy EN → **Chủ đề** tab — all 52 topic cards must show their name/counts with NO broken-image glyph. The 20 topics without icons (A1–A2 topics + Oxford) show an empty icon slot, not a broken image. (No new icons were assigned — that choice is still open.)
4. **Boss list readability:** open **Bosses** (English Hub) and **Collection**, in light AND dark theme — every **💥 Điểm yếu** chip must be clearly readable (dark-amber text on light cards in the light theme, gold on dark cards). Also check the **V19 Boss Map** zone cards — their chips must be gold on the dark purple cards in BOTH themes.
5. **Legendary fight start:** Bosses → **Legendary Boss Arena** → tap **"Khiêu chiến"** on any Legendary boss — the first question must appear immediately (~10 ms, as in V18.9 — no regression).
6. **Corrupt-save safety (only if you want to test it):** this is hard to trigger by hand — it needs a damaged `hanvocab.v1` in localStorage. If it ever happens, the game must show a warning naming the preserved copy (`hanvocab.v12.corrupt-…`) instead of silently starting fresh. Do NOT test this on a profile whose save you care about without a backup first.
