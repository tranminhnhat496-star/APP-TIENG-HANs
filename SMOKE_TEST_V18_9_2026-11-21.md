1. Open the game, go to **Bosses** (English Hub) → **Legendary Boss Arena** (Đấu Trường Huyền Thoại) and tap **"Khiêu chiến"** on any Legendary boss (e.g. Hắc Long Nham Hỏa). 3. Play a **Challenge** and an **Elemental** boss fight — both must start immediately and play as before (no change, but confirm no regression).
4. Play a **V19 Boss Map** fight — must start and play as before.
5. Check a **Korean** Legendary fight too (pick 🇰🇷) — same immediate start; with fewer than 8 Korean cards the ⚠️ banner + English fallback must still show (V40 behaviour, unchanged).
